Support Us

Help Keep Alderney Week Free

No government funding. No ticket prices. Just the generous support of people who love Alderney and what this festival means to our community.

Why Your Support Matters

Alderney Week receives no government funding. Everything you see — the sound systems, the safety barriers, the parade materials, the volunteers' support — is funded entirely through sponsorship and the generosity of people like you.

Every donation, no matter the size, makes a real difference. Your contribution helps us keep the main events completely free for families, maintain the high standards of safety and organisation the festival is known for, and ensure that Alderney Week continues to be the heartbeat of the island's summer for generations to come.

Donating is simple — just click the button below and choose the amount you'd like to give. On behalf of the whole island, thank you.

Where Your Money Goes

What Donations Fund

Every penny raised goes directly towards making Alderney Week safe, wonderful, and free.

🎺

Sound & Stage Equipment

Quality sound systems and staging for the Cavalcade, concerts, and other events throughout the week — the infrastructure that makes every event come alive.

🎠

Parade Materials

Floats, decorations, costumes, and all the materials that go into creating the spectacular Cavalcade and other parade events that are the hallmark of Alderney Week.

🦺

Safety & Security

Safety barriers, first aid provision, stewards, and all the equipment and personnel needed to ensure every event is safe and well-managed for everyone attending.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Volunteer Support

Training, equipment, and coordination for the amazing volunteers who give their time to make Alderney Week happen — the backbone of the entire festival.

🎆

Fireworks Display

The spectacular grand finale fireworks display at The Butes — one of the most memorable moments of the entire week, made possible by generous donations and sponsors.

📣

Promotion & Community

Spreading the word about Alderney Week, welcoming visitors to the island, and continuing to grow the festival so it remains relevant and exciting for new generations.

The Impact of Your Support

Thanks to generous donors and sponsors, Alderney Week continues to thrive as one of the Channel Islands' most beloved community events.
