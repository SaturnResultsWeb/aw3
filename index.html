About the Festival

Alderney's Biggest
Summer Celebration

Every summer, the tiny island of Alderney comes alive with a week of incredible events — from spectacular cavalcades and fireworks to hilarious man-powered flight contests and family beach fun.

This is community celebration at its finest — most events are completely free, and everyone is welcome, whether you're a local or visiting from across the world.

Our Story

Over a Century of Island Spirit

Alderney Week has roots stretching back to ancient island traditions — from pagan bonfire ceremonies to military sports days. The first recorded Alderney Week took place in July 1908, and it's been growing ever since.

Explore Our History
1908

The first recorded Alderney Week — sports, concerts, a torchlight tattoo and fireworks, organised by Major B.E. Ward.

1948

Post-war revival: a cattle show evolves into a carnival with beauty queens and community celebrations.

1953

The beloved cavalcade of decorated floats introduced for the very first time.

1970

Ray Parkin grows the programme to over 65 events, reaching 100+ activities by 1990.

Today

A modern festival with ancient roots — the torchlight procession reconnects us to over a thousand years of tradition.

❤️

Help Keep Alderney Week Free

Alderney Week receives no government funding. Every donation, big or small, helps cover the essentials — parade materials, sound systems, safety equipment, and the volunteers that make it all happen.
