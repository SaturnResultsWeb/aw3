1st – 8th August 2026

Events Programme

Eight days of unforgettable fun, entertainment and island spirit — something for everyone, and most events are completely free.

1 Aug

Friday 1st August

Opening day — the fun begins!

🛒

Street Market

Kick off Alderney Week at the Street Market. Local vendors fill the streets with fresh produce, handmade crafts, island souvenirs, and delicious food. A wonderful way to soak up the Alderney atmosphere and chat with locals.

All Ages Free Daytime
All Day Victoria Street
🎶

Quarry Party — Night 1

The legendary adults-only Quarry Party kicks off Alderney Week with a bang. Dance the night away in the unique setting of Alderney's historic quarry — a venue unlike any other.

18+ Only Evening
Late Night The Quarry
2 Aug

Saturday 2nd August

Cavalcade Day — the highlight of the whole week!

🎠

The Cavalcade

The undisputed highlight of Alderney Week! Beautifully decorated floats parade down Victoria Street to The Butes, with food vendors, games, and family entertainment all along the route. An unmissable community celebration packed with colour, music, and joy.

All Ages Free Daytime
Afternoon Victoria St → The Butes
3 Aug

Sunday 3rd August

Man Powered Flight — expect the unexpected!

✈️

Man Powered Flight

Competitors launch their homemade flying machines from Douglas Quay in an attempt at powered flight. The results are always wonderfully chaotic — whether they soar or splash spectacularly into the harbour below, it's guaranteed entertainment for the whole family.

All Ages Free Daytime
Afternoon Douglas Quay
🏚️

Bunker Party — Night 2

Party in one of Alderney's extraordinary WWII bunkers — an utterly unique adults-only experience. History meets nightlife in the most memorable setting you'll ever encounter.

18+ Only Evening
Late Night WWII Bunker
4 Aug

Monday 4th August

Water Splash — prepare to get soaked!

💦

Water Splash

The kids' absolute favourite! Children armed with water guns join forces — and take on — the island's fire brigade in the wettest, wildest afternoon of the week. No winners, no losers, just pure joyful chaos. Bring a change of clothes!

Kids' Favourite Free Daytime
Afternoon Town Centre
5 Aug

Tuesday 5th August

Scavenger Hunt — explore every corner of the island!

🗺️

Scavenger Hunt

Teams race around the island tracking down hidden clues and completing a list of challenges. A brilliant way to explore Alderney's beautiful coastlines, historic sites, and hidden corners — while competing to be the first team back with a completed list.

All Ages Free Daytime
All Day Island-wide
🎶

Quarry Party — Night 3

The Quarry Party returns for another unforgettable night of great music and dancing in this spectacular one-of-a-kind venue. Adults only — a true Alderney Week institution.

18+ Only Evening
Late Night The Quarry
6 Aug

Wednesday 6th August

Mid-week fun continues

🏆

Sports & Community Activities

A packed programme of sports competitions, fun games, and community activities spread throughout the day. Full schedule to be confirmed — contact the team or watch our social channels for the latest updates.

All Ages Free Daytime
All Day Various Venues
7 Aug

Thursday 7th August

Sandcastle Competition — to the beach!

🏖️

Sandcastle Competition

Head to Alderney's beautiful sandy beaches and build the most spectacular sandcastle you can! Prizes are awarded across age categories, so everyone has a chance. Pack your buckets, spades, and imagination — engineering talent and wild creativity both welcome!

All Ages Free Morning
Morning Alderney Beaches
8 Aug

Saturday 8th August

Grand Finale — Torchlight Procession & Fireworks!

🔥

Torchlight Procession, Bonfire & Fireworks

The magical grand finale of Alderney Week. Residents light their torches and walk together through the town streets, gathering at The Butes to light a great communal bonfire. An ancient tradition connecting us to over a thousand years of island history — truly moving and spectacular. The evening concludes with a breathtaking fireworks display lighting up the Alderney sky.

All Ages Free Evening
Evening Town → The Butes
🎉

Quarry Party — Grand Finale Night

End Alderney Week on the very highest note with the final Quarry Party. The biggest and best night of them all — dance, celebrate, and say a fond farewell to another incredible Alderney Week.

18+ Only Evening
Late Night The Quarry
Good to Know

Practical Information

Everything you need to enjoy Alderney Week to the fullest.

🎟️

Cost & Tickets

The vast majority of events are completely free — just turn up and enjoy! Some special activities may require a ticket. Check with the organisers for the latest details closer to the event.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Friendly

Alderney Week is a family celebration at heart. The adults-only Quarry & Bunker Parties are clearly marked — everything else is suitable for children of all ages.

📍

Getting Around

Alderney is a small island — most venues are within easy walking distance of town. Enjoy exploring on foot and discovering everything the island has to offer.
