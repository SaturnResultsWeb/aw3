The Team

Alderney Week is made possible entirely by a dedicated group of islanders who give their time, energy, and passion to make the magic happen every year.

Meet the Organisers

The People Behind Alderney Week

A dedicated team of islanders who come together each year to plan, organise, and deliver one of the Channel Islands' most-loved community festivals.

👤

Committee Chair

Overall Leadership

Leading the organising committee, overseeing the event programme, and ensuring Alderney Week remains a wonderful celebration for the whole island community.

👤

Events Coordinator

Programme & Logistics

Responsible for planning and coordinating the full events programme — from the Cavalcade to Man Powered Flight, ensuring every event runs smoothly on the day.

👤

Treasurer

Finance & Budgeting

Managing the finances of Alderney Week, ensuring that every donation and sponsorship pound is used effectively to deliver the best possible experience for everyone.

👤

Sponsorship Lead

Partners & Sponsors

Building and maintaining relationships with the generous businesses and individuals who support Alderney Week and help keep the festival free for families.

👤

Safety Officer

Health & Safety

Ensuring that every event is safe, enjoyable, and well-managed — working with authorities and volunteers to keep everyone protected throughout the week.

👤

Volunteer Coordinator

Volunteer Management

Recruiting, briefing, and supporting the amazing team of volunteers who give up their time to help make Alderney Week the spectacular event it is each year.

Want to Get Involved?

Alderney Week is run entirely by volunteers, and we're always looking for enthusiastic people to join the team. Whether you can help for a few hours or the whole week, we'd love to hear from you.

Our Ethos

What Drives Us

The values that guide everything we do at Alderney Week.

🤝

Community First

Every decision we make puts the Alderney community at its heart — locals and visitors alike, of all ages.

🆓

Free for Families

We work hard every year to ensure that the main events remain free and accessible for everyone on the island.

🏝️

Island Pride

Alderney Week celebrates everything wonderful about this unique island — its history, its people, and its spirit.

⭐

Volunteer Powered

From the committee to the stewards, Alderney Week is made possible entirely by people who simply love the island.
